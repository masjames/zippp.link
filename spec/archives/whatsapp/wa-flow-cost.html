How zippp works with WhatsApp

WhatsApp is the shutter. Google Sheet is the ledger. User sends a receipt photo (or a short note) → zippp extracts → user checks → one row lands in their Sheet. No second books. No auto-write.

Sheet = source of truth Check before Accept 1 credit = 1 Accept ≤2 outbound business msgs / Accept Soft-cap 800 outbound / mo

1. The idea in one glance

Built for personal / family cash notes (Bayu). Same Accept path as web Snap — WA just removes the “open the app” step when you already live in chat.

Job to be done

After buying something, snap the nota in WhatsApp. See the draft (merchant, amount, category). Tap Terima → it appears in the family Google Sheet. Tap Ubah to fix on web, or Buang to drop it.

Foto / teks→ Extract→ Review→ Terima→ Sheet row
WhatsApp (Meta / Twilio)
Transport only — deliver inbound photo & our 2 replies
zippp (Next.js)
Link phone ↔ Google, OCR/LLM, draft FSM, spend credit
Google Sheet (user’s)
System of record — the only place the expense “exists”
Credits (Upstash)
Money ledger — 1 Accept burns 1 credit on successful append
Not in v1 Marketing blasts, digests, Meta AI budgets, group chat bots, BSP shopping, or writing to Sheet without the user tapping Terima / typing Terima.

Path · Meta Cloud API direct

WA_PROVIDER=meta · no Twilio hop

  • Messaging: Meta Service only on 2 outbounds
  • Inbound: Meta Rp0
  • ~Rp713 / Accept after free tier
  • Faster long-term cost; slower Meta onboarding

Path · Twilio Sandbox / Twilio WA

WA_PROVIDER=twilio · calculator model

  • Twilio: $0.005 × (2 in + 2 out) = $0.02 / Accept
  • Plus Meta Service pass-through on 2 outbounds (~Rp713)
  • ~$0.02 + Rp713 after free tier · ~$0.02 while Meta free
  • Fast Sandbox today; prod still Meta-gated (WABA)

We send (keep ≤2)

  • Review + Terima / Ubah / Buang (Service or Utility QR)
  • “Tersimpan” + Open Sheet
  • Rare error / quota CTA only

We never send

  • Marketing templates
  • “Sedang dibaca” / progress spam
  • Reminders, digests, cold outreach
  • Auth OTP templates on the Accept path
