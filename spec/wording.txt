# zippp wording

Single source of truth for every user-visible string. Edit the values only.

Format: each entry is a `## key.lang` line, then the value on the following
line(s) until the next `##`. `en` is English (US), `id` is Indonesian.
Do not put a `##` at the start of a value line.

Prices are regional: Indonesian copy (id) quotes IDR, English (intl) quotes USD.
`/` is English (international), `/id` is Indonesian.

# Google user data we receive

- Your Google account id and email address. We use them to identify your account, to send the emails you ask for, and to check admin access on our server.
- A Google OAuth refresh token. It is the key that lets zippp write to the sheet you connect, and it is encrypted on our servers.
- The spreadsheet id and tab names of the sheet you connect, plus its first row. We read the first row to map your columns.
- With the Drive file permission, only files zippp created or you picked here: we read the file id and name to find your [zippp] sheet.

We do not read your data rows and we do not browse your Drive.

# How we use it, per permission

- spreadsheets: write the rows you accept, and the header row when zippp creates the sheet. Read the first row and the tab names only.
- drive.file: find or create your [zippp] spreadsheet, so you do not have to paste a link.
- userinfo.email: identify you, keep sessions separate, send account emails, and decide admin access on the server.

We do not use Google user data for advertising or profiling, and we do not use it to train AI models.

# What never goes to an AI provider

The photo you take is not Google user data, and the two paths stay apart in code:

- The photo goes to Baidu AI Studio (PaddleOCR) to read text, then to DeepSeek to structure it. Gemini is the backup if those fail.
- Those providers receive only that one photo, or the text read from it. They never receive your Google account data, your sheet contents, your spreadsheet id, or your Drive files.
- The read path does not load your Google token at all.

# Who we share data with

We do not sell your data, and we do not share it with advertisers or data brokers. We share it only with the providers that run zippp, and only as far as each one needs it:

- Google. The Sheets and Drive calls described above.
- Vercel. Hosting and server logs.
- Upstash. Encrypted storage for your account record, token, sheet setup, and credit ledger.
- Mailtrap. Sends the emails you ask for, to the address on your account.
- Telegram. Admin alerts with order number, email, and amount. No sheet contents.
- Paddle and Kasera Pay. Payments. They do not receive Google user data.

If the law requires disclosure, we disclose and we tell you unless we are barred from telling you. If zippp changes operator through a sale or merger, records move with it under this policy.

# How we protect it

- TLS for every request and every API call.
- OAuth tokens are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM, using a key held only in server environment variables. Tokens never reach your browser.
- Least privilege: three permissions, spreadsheets, drive.file, and your email address. No Gmail, no full Drive, no contacts.
- Admin pages check the signed-in email on the server and return 404 for anyone else.
- Analytics record page views and basic device information. Text you type, receipt values, and sheet contents are not sent.
- Production records are reachable only by the operator.

# Limited Use

zippp's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

# What we store

- Your Google account id and email.

- Your credit ledger: top ups, spends, refunds, referral rewards, and payment order records.
- Extraction logs: timings and which provider answered. No receipt values and no images.
- Correction counts when you accept a card: a short one-way hash of your user id, which fields you edited, and whether they were flagged. No values and no images.

- Receipt photos. The image is processed and dropped. It lives in your browser only until the rows are sent.
- Receipt rows. They go to your Google Sheet and nowhere else.
- Card numbers or wallet credentials. Payments are handled by the payment provider.

zippp runs on Vercel. Account records are stored in Upstash Redis. Indonesian top ups go through Kasera Pay (QRIS), with a manual GoPay transfer as a fallback. # Menghapus data Anda

Keluar di Pengaturan: token yang tersimpan dan pengaturan sheet langsung dihapus dari server kami. Untuk mencabut akses zippp di Google juga, cabut di [izin Google](https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). Untuk menghapus catatan akun dan buku kredit, kirim email dari alamat Google yang dipakai di akun Anda (lihat halaman kontak). Baris di sheet Anda adalah milik Anda dan tidak pernah dihapus zippp.

## legal.refund.body.en
Credits are prepaid and digital. This page says when money or credit comes back.

# Automatic

- A photo that cannot be read costs nothing.
- If sending to your sheet fails after the credit was taken, the credit is returned automatically.
- A payment page that is not paid within 60 minutes expires and nothing is charged.

# On request

Email us within 7 days (see the contact page) of the payment if:

- you were charged twice for one top-up,
- you paid but credits were not added after verification,
- you were charged an amount you did not choose.

We refund to the payment method you used, or add the missing credits, within 7 working days of confirming the issue.

# Not refunded

- Credits that were used to send rows.
- Credits that expired after {ttlDays} days.
- Change of mind after the credits were added, unless required by law.

# Otomatis

- Foto yang tidak terbaca tidak dikenai biaya.
- Jika pengiriman ke sheet gagal setelah kredit terpakai, kredit dikembalikan otomatis.
- Halaman pembayaran yang tidak dibayar dalam 60 menit kedaluwarsa dan tidak ada tagihan.

# Atas permintaan

Email kami dalam 7 hari (lihat halaman kontak) sejak pembayaran jika:

- Anda tertagih dua kali untuk satu isi saldo,
- Anda sudah bayar tetapi kredit tidak masuk setelah diverifikasi,
- Anda tertagih nominal yang tidak Anda pilih.

Kami mengembalikan ke metode pembayaran yang Anda pakai, atau menambahkan kredit yang kurang, dalam 7 hari kerja setelah masalah dikonfirmasi.

# Tidak dikembalikan

- Kredit yang sudah dipakai untuk mengirim baris.
- Kredit yang kedaluwarsa setelah {ttlDays} hari.
- Berubah pikiran setelah kredit masuk, kecuali diwajibkan hukum.
