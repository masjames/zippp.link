---

## One sentence

**Zippp Space** is your personal finance OS — record spend (scan, manual, or notification prompt), track assets and goals, share a Space with anyone you trust. Pay monthly on the web.

---

## Who we sell to

| Who | Pitch |
|---|---|
| You (personal) | Photograph or log every spend; day/month totals; goals and assets in one Space. |
| You + people you trust | Invite a partner or housemate into a **Zippp Space** — not “family-only.” |

**Not the ICP anymore:** restaurant purchasing, sales recap, staff reimbursement mills.

---

## What we sell now (direction)

- **Monthly subscription** with a **fair-use usage %** meter (Claude Pro / Max style).
- **Checkout = web only.** Android never sells plans or packs.
- **Capture:** camera scan, manual entry, proactive remind via notification listener (opt-in).
- **Zippp Space:** shared personal-finance workspace.
- **Assets + financial goals** are in-product (ROAD phases after capture loop).
- Google Sheet remains a landing surface for spend rows while the Space product expands; Sheet is not the whole product forever — see DIR-001.

Legacy prepaid credits may still exist in code until ROAD Phase 6. Do not design new UX around credits.

---

## Why subscription + fair use

- A finance OS is ongoing, not a scan vending machine.
- Usage % is legible; prepaid packs train the wrong habit.
- Web pay keeps Play distribution honest (no IAP).

---

## Privacy (unchanged spine)

Receipt photos → OCR / structure on server paths already defined; optional proof in the user’s Drive. No silent bank-notification cloud dumps (DEC-004).

---

## Success

A real spend lands in the user’s Space (and sheet row when connected) without retyping — including when Zippp nudged them after a bank alert.
