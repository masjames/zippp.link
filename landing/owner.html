Payment verification

Per project, set in the project editor. Never stores or shows amounts, banking, transaction or payer data. Three honest states:

Payment verified You hold independent proof the client paid. No details shown publicly.
Self-reported A client reported a value in a verification. Labeled self-reported everywhere.
Not disclosed The default. No payment claim is made.
